# Welcome to Credaxis 🎓🔗
A decentralized transcript and credential verification marketplace built for students, institutions, and employers. Credaxis acts as a neutral intermediary that enables secure issuance, ownership, sharing, and verification of academic records using blockchain technology.

## 🏦 What We Offer
Credaxis modernizes academic credential management through decentralization and trustless verification:
- **Decentralized Transcripts**: Institutions issue tamper-proof transcripts and credentials recorded on-chain with decentralized storage.
- **Instant Verification**: Employers and third parties verify academic records instantly without manual back-and-forth.
- **Student Ownership**: Learners fully control who can access and verify their credentials.
- **Credential Marketplace**: A shared platform connecting universities, students, and companies for transcript access and verification.
